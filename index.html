Curator’s note

A living archive that keeps the past intact.

The first version of Sofoste felt like an open notebook. It brought learning projects, musical ideas, personal interests and an image gallery together in a very simple HTML website.

This restoration preserves its handmade energy and old-time character while making it clearer, more accessible and better suited to today’s screens. Personal details, discontinued services and obsolete links have been removed.
